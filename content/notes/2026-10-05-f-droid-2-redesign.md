**Logged at IST:** 2026-10-05 14:38 IST

**What it is:** F-Droid 2.0 is the largest update to its official Android app in ten years, rebuilt with Kotlin Compose.

**Gist:** The app reorganizes its main navigation into Discover, Search, and My Apps. The release expands categories, adds app-description and translated-content search, improves Chinese, Japanese, and Korean search, and adds combined filters for category, device compatibility, and anti-features. F-Droid says the redesign aims to improve discovery without tracking users or optimizing for engagement. The rollout follows fourteen test releases.

**Why it matters:** Open app distribution is not only a repository problem. People also need to find relevant software, understand what they are installing, and manage updates. F-Droid 2.0 treats those everyday product details as part of making an independent app ecosystem usable.

**Context:** The project’s homepage also links its current campaign about proposed changes to Android app installation. This note focuses on the independently announced app redesign, not a prediction about that policy dispute.
