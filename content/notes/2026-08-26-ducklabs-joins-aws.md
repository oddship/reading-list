**Logged at IST:** 2026-08-26 21:45 IST

**What it is:** DuckLabs' announcement that it is joining AWS, shared by Frank Denis.

**Gist:** DuckLabs says the Amsterdam team behind DuckDB will stay together under AWS, continuing work on DuckDB, DuckLake, Quack, and the wider Duck Stack. The company is presenting this as a scale move: DuckDB now has more than one million downloads a day, and DuckLabs says AWS gives it the resources, infrastructure, and customer reach to grow without turning the small team into a large sales and support organization.

The trust question is the important part. DuckLabs says DuckDB and the other open-source components will remain free and MIT-licensed, with the nonprofit DuckDB Foundation continuing stewardship. It also says the foundation will add a technical advisory board, and the team plans to open the extension stack so extensions signed by other developers and organizations can run in DuckDB.

This is good news if AWS really accelerates the engineering while leaving DuckDB's open core and foundation governance intact. It is also the kind of acquisition that deserves follow-up: DuckDB sits in a vendor-neutral spot in the analytics ecosystem, and that neutrality is exactly why a lot of people trust it.

**Newsletter angle:** Significant systems/open-source infrastructure item: a vendor-neutral local analytics engine gets a hyperscaler home while trying to preserve foundation governance and open licensing.
