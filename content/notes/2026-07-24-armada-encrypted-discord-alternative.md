**Logged at IST:** 2026-07-24 18:10 IST

**What it is:** Soapbox launch/explainer for Armada, an open-source, end-to-end encrypted Discord-style community chat app built on Nostr and the Concord protocol.

**Gist:** Armada is pitched as an answer to several Discord pain points: centralized bans, unencrypted text history, ID and face-scan verification pressure, data breaches, ads, and IPO-driven monetization. Instead of a company-issued account, identity is a Nostr key pair. Communities are end-to-end encrypted, publish encrypted messages to multiple independent relays, and can keep running if a relay disappears or removes them.

The product is still in open beta, but it already aims for Discord familiarity: servers, channels, threads, reactions, roles, voice/video, screen sharing, events, bots, slash commands, GIFs, emoji, and themes. The more interesting systems bits are no email/phone/ID signup, self-hostable relays, Android Bluetooth mesh mode, Android notifications without Google push, and an identity that can outlive the app across the Nostr ecosystem.

**Newsletter angle:** Useful security and systems item because it turns the “encrypted Discord alternative” idea into a concrete decentralized protocol and product stack, with tradeoffs around keys, relays, moderation, and early beta rough edges.
