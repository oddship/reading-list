WIRED traces lowercase writing from early computing, where terminals often could not display lowercase letters, through internet subcultures and today's social platforms. The style has carried different meanings: feminine-coded informality and vulnerability in spaces shaped by Tumblr and Instagram, and a long-running norm in communities like 4chan where punctuation conventions grew around plain text.

The essay's point is that lowercase is not one coherent generational signal. Its meaning shifts with its community and history, even as young writers use it to soften self-presentation or make personal writing feel less polished. The same visual choice can signal intimacy, irony, habit, or simply the constraints of an older technical era.
